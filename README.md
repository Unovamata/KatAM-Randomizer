# KatAM Randomizer
 
Still in development, thank you for your patience.
This randomizer supports the Japanese (`AGB-B8KJ`), American (`AGB-B8KE`) and European (`AGB-B8KP`) versions of Kirby and the Amazing Mirror. The region is detected when the ROM is loaded.

- **Save Settings / Load Settings** store every option and the seed in a `.json` file, to reuse or share a configuration.
- With **Spoiler Log** ticked, randomizing also writes `KatAM - Spoiler Log.txt` next to the randomized ROM: the settings, every mirror and where it leads now, where the Boss & Goal game rooms are reached from, and the enemies, mini-bosses, chests, items, ability pedestals and map elements that changed.
- **Random Intro Parade** (Miscellaneous > Game): the characters parading through the intro, after the HAL Laboratory / Nintendo / Flagship logos, are swapped for others of the same kind (small and big walkers, fliers), including 7 the intro never shows, and, with the ROM Expansion, 7 of the ported NiDL enemies (Blade Knight, Needlous, Starman and Gip walking, Twizzy, Cool Spook and Bubbles flying, each playing its NiDL walk or flight); each character is swapped for a different one wherever it appears, so the parade never needs more sprite palettes or VRAM than the game has. See `KatAMTitleScreen.cs`.
- **Reduce Flashing** (Miscellaneous > Game): KatAM's white screen flashes (Kracko's lightning, explosions, Dark Mind, Crash, Light and Mike...) brighten the screen at most a step a frame, as its fades do, instead of all at once; fades look the same. It works with or without the ROM Expansion (the ROM is padded to 32 MB either way). See `ROM Expansion/calm.c`.
- **Skip the Tutorial** (Miscellaneous > Game): a game starts as a continued save does, in Central Circle where loading a save puts Kirby, with no opening cutscene, no tutorial and no cutscene after it (a save made by another ROM, vanilla or an older randomized one, or saved mid-tutorial, too), with what the tutorial's big chest holds (the Rainbow Route map, or what the randomizer put there), so nothing is lost: the chest counts as opened. It works with or without the ROM Expansion. See `ROM Expansion/skip.c`.
- **Unlock the Master Sword** (Miscellaneous > Game): the Master Sword's stand in Central Circle works from a new game, not only once the game is beaten (KatAM's two checks dropped, `KatAMExpansionCalm.cs`). It works with or without the ROM Expansion.
- **Unlock the Ability Room** (Miscellaneous > Game): Central Circle's door to the ability room is open from a new game (or an old save), not only once the 15 big switches are pressed; with the ROM Expansion the NiDL hub's copy of the door is open too. It works with or without the ROM Expansion. See `ROM Expansion/unlock.c`.
- **Mirror shards** (Chests & Items > Mirror Shards): two options, separately or both at once. **Field shards** (Unchanged, Random or Custom, 1-2): each step adds one more copy of all 8 shards, lying in rooms, each replacing a random loose item; one already owned is a Maxim Tomato instead (as a boss fought again leaves), so it never blocks the way. **Add Shards in Chests** (off by default, 1-2): each step adds one more copy of all 8 shards, put into existing chests that held a consumable (never a new chest, never a treasure); opening one collects its shard right away and lets the shard out. Only a boss's shard takes Kirby out of the level: a shard from a chest or a room plays its cutscene and Kirby plays on where he is, with no victory dance. **No Chests** is above all of it: no chests at all, so no shards in chests (the option is greyed out). Shard chests are left out of the pause screen's chest lists. It works with or without the ROM Expansion, in the USA, European and Japanese versions. See `KatAMItems.cs` and `ROM Expansion/chest.c`.
- **Bosses Give Random Shards** (Chests & Items > Mirror Shards, off by default): which mirror shard each boss leaves is shuffled, so area 1's boss may leave area 8's shard. KatAM's 8 bosses leave all 8 shards between them (the game is beaten as before), then the ROM Expansion's NiDL bosses leave them again, spread as evenly as they go (the 7 that leave one, not the finale's Power Orb and Nightmare: each shard once at most). A boss whose shard is owned, from anywhere, leaves a Maxim Tomato, as before; the pause screen's area maps show the area boss's new shard (without the ROM Expansion: with it the pause screen has no area maps); the victory dance stays where it is in each boss room. The spoiler log lists who leaves what (Boss Mirror Shards). See `KatAMExpansionChests.cs` and `ROM Expansion/chest.c`.
- **NiDL bosses** (ROM Expansion): the NiDL levels' boss rooms get their bosses back, ported from NiDL, each leaving the mirror shard of its level when beaten (the shard cutscene, then the Dimension Mirror; a Maxim Tomato if that shard is owned already), its room's exit opening then; but the Fountain of Dreams', the finale before KatAM's final boss, which leave none. Every NiDL boss is ported: Whispy Woods (Vegetable Valley, shard 1): its face blinks, it blows puffs of air along the ground and drops apples that bounce and roll at Kirby, which he inhales and spits back at it. Kracko (Grape Garden, shard 4): its cloud comes down on the cloud tower's bottom screen, its eye following Kirby; it bobs, sweeps across, darkens the screen and crosses it striking lightning down to the floor, swoops in a loop down at Kirby, and drops Waddle Does (Kirby's way to a power to hit it with). Mr. Shine & Mr. Bright (Butter Building, shard 3): one fights on the ground while the other attacks from the sky, trading places every 15 seconds; Mr. Bright rolls in flames, punches fireballs and jumps, Mr. Shine throws crescents that come back and jumps, the moon drops falling stars (which Kirby inhales and spits back) and the sun shines a beam down at Kirby. Meta Knight (Orange Ocean, shard 6): Kirby fights him with whatever powers he brings (no sword is given, no statue set down); he waits a moment in his cape, then duels with Galaxia: he walks up and slashes, dashes across with his sword out, and leaps at Kirby slashing as he lands. Paint Roller (Ice Cream Island, shard 2), NiDL's fight 1:1: he skates and jumps from easel to easel and paints, and what he painted comes to life: a car, a Kirby, a Waddle Dee or a microphone running at Kirby, a baseball flying at him, a bomb that hops over and blows up, a cloud that drifts after him dropping lightning, or a parasol floating after him; Kirby inhales them for their powers (the car Wheel, the microphone Mike, the baseball Ball, the bomb Crash, the cloud Spark, the parasol Parasol). Heavy Mole (Yogurt Yard, shard 5): it digs up and down on the right of its screen, reaching its two spiked arms out at Kirby and firing their balls at him, which fly back; stars fall from the ceiling as it digs, for Kirby to inhale and spit at it. King Dedede (Rainbow Resort, shard 7): he walks at Kirby and, between rests, dives at him on his belly, jumps high and lands on him (stars burst out for Kirby to inhale), inhales, swallowing Kirby and his helpers in front of him and spitting them out in a star that bursts on the wall, or swings his hammer down one to three times. Nightmare (the Fountain of Dreams), both of NiDL's fights, the finale before KatAM's final boss: Central Circle's Dimension Mirror and the NiDL hub's lead into it once KatAM's 8 shards are found (nothing else does). The Power Orb is NiDL's Star Rod flight: NiDL's intro first (Kirby and every helper fly up through space from the room's bottom to the orb, the screen rising with them, Kirby swings at it and it loops round to its spot), then Kirby and every helper fly through space with the Star Rod and shooting stars as the screen sinks, while the orb hovers, roams in a figure of eight, charges and dashes, and zips up and down, firing stars and crescents; beaten, it bursts, comes back and flies off as in NiDL, and a warp star comes where it was; the helpers fly to it by themselves, it leaves once every Kirby is on it, and every Kirby rides it as in NiDL, up off the screen as the screen rises to the moon, the orb and then the star shrinking into it, to Nightmare's room, keeping the Star Rod: as in NiDL, Nightmare is fought with it on the ground (Kirby walks, runs, jumps and floats holding the rod, and B swings it to shoot a star; a hit doesn't take it away). Nightmare, on the floor at the bottom of his room, floats above him, firing fans of stars, sending beams along the floor, swooping down and vanishing to come back on the other side; beaten, he flies off, turns to light, comes back and breaks up into light as in NiDL, bursts go off along the floor, and after a fade to white NiDL's ending down from the moon plays in the Power Orb's room, without its narration (the moon bursts into a crescent, the warp star with the Kirbys flies out of it and down through the night sky and the clouds to Dream Land's dusk, King Dedede flying by), until the star stops, before Kirby jumps off to throw the Star Rod; then, after a fade to white, the Kirbys land in the Fountain of Dreams hub with the abilities they had before the Power Orb, a room with no other door in or out, no big switch and no ability statues: an Amazing Mirror to KatAM's final boss high in its middle (whole once the 8 shards are found, as Central Circle's was) and a Maximum Tomato under it. In Nightmare's room the star flies the Kirbys down as the screen scrolls to the fight, as in NiDL, his comet waking up far off in the sky with the screen flashing (steady with "Reduce Flashing"), then throwing blue stars at the star as it dodges until one hits it: the Kirbys tumble off to the floor and, back on their feet, see Nightmare's light grow into his shape before the fight starts. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`, Bosses.
- **Mini-bosses stay on screen**: a randomized mini-boss fights inside an area the camera locks onto (KatAM's mini-bosses and the ported ones keep themselves inside it). Mini-bosses mixed into the enemies ("Include Minibosses" in the enemy options) can take any enemy spot; their area is the whole room, so the camera doesn't lock and Kirby can walk away from them. Batafire, which flies across its arena from one side, is moved to the side of the arena its new spot was nearer to; Bombar faces into the room, so it only flies off screen with its own exit dive.
- **Pause screen** (with the ROM Expansion only; other ROMs keep every pause screen): every ability shows the base pause help screen ("About Copy Abilities"; English in every language of the European version), still with the current ability's picture. The other screens' graphics (about 480 KB) are wiped to free room for gameplay additions. SELECT switches between that screen and the world map, the big map of all areas: the area maps are gone (the Map Viewer shows each area's rooms), and their data, the backgrounds behind them, their room layouts, frame, area titles and shard and map room tiles, is wiped too (about 61 KB; 76 KB in the European version). The room squares, lines and colors the Map Viewer draws with stay. See `KatAMPauseScreens.cs`.
- **ROM Expansion** (USA only) adds enemies from *Kirby: Nightmare in Dream Land* to the enemy pool. Load your own Nightmare in Dream Land (USA, `AGB-A7KE`) ROM in the ROM Expansion tab: the graphics are taken from it, nothing from that game ships with the randomizer. The new enemies appear when enemies are randomized.
  - **Mini-bosses**: Poppy Bros. Sr., Mr. Tick-Tock, Grand Wheelie, Bugzzy, Fire Lion and the Meta-Knights, ported from Nightmare in Dream Land, joins the mini-boss pool with "Include Mini-Bosses" (it appears when mini-bosses are randomized), with the HP bar, music and camera of KatAM's own mini-bosses. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`.
  - **Title screen** ("Random Title Screen: KatAM's Intro or NiDL's Kirby Swarm"): the seed picks KatAM's intro or Nightmare in Dream Land's title animation, its 115 pink Kirbys running, walking, floating and tumbling across the logos exactly as in NiDL (their scripts, poses and palette read from your NiDL ROM), KatAM's title screen fading in under them. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`.
  - **Rooms** ("Include Rooms"): Nightmare in Dream Land's level rooms (their foreground, background, collision, breakable blocks, enemies, mini-bosses and items, with KatAM's or the ported enemies) are added to the ROM, read from your own NiDL ROM, each level as an area of its own. A pink mirror on the left of Central Circle's platform leads to the NiDL hub, a second Central Circle at night under NiDL's final boss sky, with Central Circle's Dimension Mirror and a door per level in one of KatAM's door designs Central Circle doesn't use. As in Central Circle, the hub's golden mirror is the way in: it leads to Vegetable Valley's first room, from which every NiDL room can be reached (each boss stage leads to the next level); the doors are shortcuts, and a pink mirror leads back to Central Circle. Each level has a switch room laid out like KatAM's rooms with two hub doors, in its own tiles, with a pedestal, two ability statues and one big switch: pressing it plays the world map animation of the level's new path, opens its door in the NiDL hub (hidden until then, as KatAM's), and opens the room's two hub doors: one back to the NiDL hub, and one to where its path leads (another level, the hub, or a KatAM hub room, which gets a door back; never more than 2 in a row). Each level is laid out as KatAM's areas are: its stages branch off each other through two-way doors in the middle of rooms or hidden away (big stages branch more), loop back and link to other levels and to a KatAM area through one-way doors that throw Kirby around (KatAM's own rooms keep all their doors: mirrors are only added), and end in one-way goal mirrors to KatAM's goal games (back to Central Circle after), another level or KatAM; each boss stage (one-way, as KatAM's) leads to the next level's first room, and the Fountain of Dreams back to the hub. Each switch room is reached through a Mirra hidden somewhere in its level: as few as KatAM's (one a level), one-way, never two in a row (Mirras, KatAM's too, fall once what they stand on breaks, and open their door where they land); the switch rooms' and the hub's doors are always two-way. Doors follow KatAM's rules: at most 3 a room (2 in small ones) and never 3 in the camera's view (NiDL's own rooms of doors keep theirs, as NiDL's doorways, without mirrors), a two-way door lands Kirby on the door back to where he came from, one-way doors show KatAM's one-way mirror and never drop Kirby in front of a door, goal rooms keep their goal mirror alone, no door is added in a mini-boss's arena (its doors open once it's beaten, and doors into its room are one-way), and no enemy waits where a door lands Kirby. NiDL's own doors are KatAM mirrors placed where the camera shows them; its warp stars and cannons (with their fuses, lit by fire) are KatAM's own. Each room has its own door list, so mirrors no longer slow the game down. Backgrounds pan across once per room (and stay still in rooms the camera hardly moves in), the camera shows NiDL's whole top row (a row down where it only repeats the one below), mini-boss fights hold the camera on their screen, ladders climb as KatAM's, NiDL's grey metal blocks only break with the abilities KatAM's do (nothing they guard is needed to get anywhere), thin floors can be dropped through as in NiDL (where that never cuts a door off), blocks a door would land Kirby inside start broken, and enemies NiDL put inside walls stand in the open. NiDL's own hubs and the unused rooms are left out. The map tracker shows the new rooms. The rooms also get chests, made with them (randomized or not), as KatAM places its own: a few per level (30 in all), one a room at most, at reward spots (a loose item behind breakable blocks or in a dead end becomes a chest holding it; a dead end with none gets a new one, far into the room, and an empty dead end always does), never in a boss room, a switch room or a mini-boss's fight area; they open once and stay open, and the game's completion still counts only KatAM's chests. Batteries for the phone lie at rewarding spots too (behind breakable blocks, at the end of dead ends), and the rooms' items and batteries are taken once per visit, as KatAM's. The randomizer reads the new rooms' items and chests with KatAM's, so they're randomized, shuffled, emptied by No Chests and used by the chest shards like any other. **Unload** in the ROM Expansion tab forgets the NiDL ROM. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`.
  - **New copy abilities**: Needle, Freeze, Light, Ball, Hi-Jump, Mike and Backdrop, from the Nightmare in Dream Land enemies and mini-bosses that give them (Needlous, Chilly, Cool Spook, Bubbles, Starman, Mr. Tick-Tock, Bugzzy), with Nightmare in Dream Land's poses, hats and ability cards. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`.
  - **Broom Hatter**: its behavior was re-implemented from Nightmare in Dream Land's code (sweep timings, speeds, frames, broom dust, random choice between sweeping and sweeping back and forth, stopping at walls and ledges). It uses the unused object type `0x31` and has its own name on the HP bar. The source is in `ROM Expansion/`.
