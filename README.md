# KatAM Randomizer
 
Still in development, thank you for your patience.
This randomizer supports the Japanese (`AGB-B8KJ`), American (`AGB-B8KE`) and European (`AGB-B8KP`) versions of Kirby and the Amazing Mirror. The region is detected when the ROM is loaded.

- **Save Settings / Load Settings** store every option and the seed in a `.json` file, to reuse or share a configuration.
- With **Spoiler Log** ticked, randomizing also writes `KatAM - Spoiler Log.txt` next to the randomized ROM: the settings, every mirror and where it leads now, where the Boss & Goal game rooms are reached from, and the enemies, mini-bosses, chests, items, ability pedestals and map elements that changed.
- **Random intro parade** (General Options): the characters parading through the intro, after the HAL Laboratory / Nintendo / Flagship logos, are swapped for others of the same kind (small and big walkers, fliers), including 7 the intro never shows; each character is swapped for a different one wherever it appears, so the parade never needs more sprite palettes or VRAM than the game has. See `KatAMTitleScreen.cs`.
- **Reduce flashing** (General Options): KatAM's white screen flashes (Kracko's lightning, explosions, Dark Mind, Crash, Light and Mike...) brighten the screen at most a step a frame, as its fades do, instead of all at once; fades look the same. It works with or without the ROM Expansion (the ROM is padded to 32 MB either way). See `ROM Expansion/calm.c`.
- **Mini-bosses stay on screen**: a randomized mini-boss fights inside an area the camera locks onto (KatAM's mini-bosses and the ported ones keep themselves inside it). Mini-bosses mixed into the enemies ("Include mini-bosses" in the enemy options) can take any enemy spot; their area is the whole room, so the camera doesn't lock and Kirby can walk away from them. Batafire, which flies across its arena from one side, is moved to the side of the arena its new spot was nearer to; Bombar faces into the room, so it only flies off screen with its own exit dive.
- **Pause screen** (with the ROM Expansion only; other ROMs keep every pause screen): every ability shows the base pause help screen ("About Copy Abilities"; English in every language of the European version), still with the current ability's picture. The other screens' graphics (about 480 KB) are wiped to free room for gameplay additions.
- **ROM Expansion** (USA only) adds enemies from *Kirby: Nightmare in Dream Land* to the enemy pool. Load your own Nightmare in Dream Land (USA, `AGB-A7KE`) ROM in the ROM Expansion tab: the graphics are taken from it, nothing from that game ships with the randomizer. The new enemies appear when enemies are randomized.
  - **Mini-bosses**: Poppy Bros. Sr., Mr. Tick-Tock, Grand Wheelie, Bugzzy, Fire Lion and the Meta-Knights, ported from Nightmare in Dream Land, joins the mini-boss pool with "Include mini-bosses" (it appears when mini-bosses are randomized), with the HP bar, music and camera of KatAM's own mini-bosses. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`.
  - **Title screen** ("Title screen: KatAM's intro or NiDL's Kirby swarm"): the seed picks KatAM's intro or Nightmare in Dream Land's title animation, its 115 pink Kirbys running, walking, floating and tumbling across the logos exactly as in NiDL (their scripts, poses and palette read from your NiDL ROM), KatAM's title screen fading in under them. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`.
  - **Rooms** ("Include rooms"): Nightmare in Dream Land's level rooms (their foreground, background, collision, breakable blocks, enemies, mini-bosses and items, with KatAM's or the ported enemies) are added to the ROM, read from your own NiDL ROM, each level as an area of its own. A pink mirror on the left of Central Circle's platform leads to the NiDL hub, a second Central Circle at night under NiDL's final boss sky, with Central Circle's Dimension Mirror and a door per level in one of KatAM's door designs Central Circle doesn't use. As in Central Circle, the hub's golden mirror is the way in: it leads to Vegetable Valley's first room, from which every NiDL room can be reached (each boss stage leads to the next level); the doors are shortcuts, and a pink mirror leads back to Central Circle. Each level has a switch room laid out like KatAM's rooms with two hub doors, in its own tiles, with a pedestal, two ability statues and one big switch: pressing it plays the world map animation of the level's new path, opens its door in the NiDL hub (hidden until then, as KatAM's), and opens the room's two hub doors: one back to the NiDL hub, and one to where its path leads (another level, the hub, or a KatAM hub room, which gets a door back; never more than 2 in a row). Each level is laid out as KatAM's areas are: its stages branch off each other through two-way doors in the middle of rooms or hidden away (big stages branch more), loop back and link to other levels and to a KatAM area through one-way doors that throw Kirby around (KatAM's own rooms keep all their doors: mirrors are only added), and end in one-way goal mirrors to KatAM's goal games (back to Central Circle after), another level or KatAM; each boss stage (one-way, as KatAM's) leads to the next level's first room, and the Fountain of Dreams back to the hub. Each switch room is reached through a Mirra hidden somewhere in its level: as few as KatAM's (one a level), one-way, never two in a row; the switch rooms' and the hub's doors are always two-way. Doors follow KatAM's rules: at most 3 a room (2 in small ones) and never 3 in the camera's view (NiDL's own rooms of doors keep theirs, as NiDL's doorways, without mirrors), a two-way door lands Kirby on the door back to where he came from, one-way doors show KatAM's one-way mirror and never drop Kirby in front of a door, goal rooms keep their goal mirror alone, no door is added in a mini-boss's arena (its doors open once it's beaten, and doors into its room are one-way), and no enemy waits where a door lands Kirby. NiDL's own doors are KatAM mirrors placed where the camera shows them; its warp stars and cannons (with their fuses, lit by fire) are KatAM's own. Each room has its own door list, so mirrors no longer slow the game down. Backgrounds pan across once per room (and stay still in rooms the camera hardly moves in), the camera shows NiDL's whole top row (a row down where it only repeats the one below), mini-boss fights hold the camera on their screen, ladders climb as KatAM's, NiDL's grey metal blocks only break with the abilities KatAM's do (nothing they guard is needed to get anywhere), thin floors can be dropped through as in NiDL (where that never cuts a door off), blocks a door would land Kirby inside start broken, and enemies NiDL put inside walls stand in the open. NiDL's own hubs and the unused rooms are left out. The map tracker shows the new rooms. **Unload** in the ROM Expansion tab forgets the NiDL ROM. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`.
  - **New copy abilities**: Needle, Freeze, Light, Ball, Hi-Jump, Mike and Backdrop, from the Nightmare in Dream Land enemies and mini-bosses that give them (Needlous, Chilly, Cool Spook, Bubbles, Starman, Mr. Tick-Tock, Bugzzy), with Nightmare in Dream Land's poses, hats and ability cards. See `ROM Expansion/README.md`.
  - **Broom Hatter**: its behavior was re-implemented from Nightmare in Dream Land's code (sweep timings, speeds, frames, broom dust, random choice between sweeping and sweeping back and forth, stopping at walls and ledges). It uses the unused object type `0x31` and has its own name on the HP bar. The source is in `ROM Expansion/`.
